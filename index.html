🌸 Fascinating Pollinator Facts

  • 🐝 Bees can recognize human faces!
  • 🌼 A single bee can visit up to 5,000 flowers in one day.
  • 🦋 Butterflies taste with their feet.
  • 🐞 Ladybugs can eat up to 5,000 aphids in their lifetime.
  • 🦇 Bats are important pollinators for many night-blooming plants.
  • 🐦 Hummingbirds can visit 1,000–2,000 flowers per day.
  • 🌲 The Pacific Northwest is home to over 500 native bee species.
  • 🍏 Mason bees are super-efficient pollinators — just 250–300 can pollinate an acre of apple trees!

About the Author

Kimmy Some Love Smith is a spirited author and creator who brings her love of the natural world to life on every page. She is devoted to biodiversity, organic gardening, and the protection of pollinators. She encourages families to reconnect with the land through native seeds, plant education, and sustainable gardening practices. Her number one goal is to share the value of permaculture, environmental protection, and seed preservation, showing how small choices — like planting local species or saving heirloom seeds — can protect ecosystems, strengthen food security, and build resilient communities. Kimmy’s work sparks curiosity and empowers children to become joyful stewards of the Earth — all while having a blast coloring!

Kimmy Some Love Books:

🌲 Earth’s Pollinators, Insects, and Beneficial Friends of The Pacific Northwest — Step into the misty forests, wild coastlines, and mountain meadows of the Pacific Northwest with this engaging ABC-style coloring book. From Anna’s Hummingbird to the Zinnia-loving pollinators, children will discover a vibrant cast of creatures that call this region home. 🌵 Earth’s Pollinators, Insects, and Beneficial Friends of The Southwest — Journey through sunlit deserts, dramatic canyons, and night-blooming landscapes in this ABC-style coloring book. Kids will be amazed to learn how life adapts to the challenges of heat, drought, and intense sunlight — from the resilient prickly pear cactus to lizards darting between shade. This Southwest edition also features bonus riddles woven throughout, adding a new level of fun and discovery to every page. Packed with facts, activities, and richly detailed illustrations, it’s both a playful coloring adventure and an educational journey through one of America’s most diverse and surprising ecosystems.

🌈 Our Mission

🌱 We donate 42¢ of every sale 🌈

The number 42 in our name represents the meaning of life— inspired by the beautiful fact that when sunlight hits rain droplets at a 42-degree angle, it creates a rainbow.

We donate 42¢ of every sale to support organizations, schools, and non-profits dedicated to organic farming, pollinator protection, and ecological stewardship. 🌱 Every contribution helps nurture biodiversity and safeguard the health of our beautiful Earth.

At KimmySomeLove42, we believe in the importance of pollinators for our ecosystem and food supply. That’s why we’re committed to supporting organizations that educate, protect, and preserve these vital creatures and promote sustainable, organic farming practices.

Through your purchase, you’re not just getting a fun and educational coloring book — you’re also contributing to the conservation of pollinators, the beauties of biodiversity, and the education of permaculture.
